The challenge

Talent is everywhere. Information is not.

The barrier is often not ability or ambition. It’s knowing what exists, who can apply and how.

Scattered information

Announcements are spread across social media, messaging groups, school networks and separate websites.

Missed deadlines

Students often find out about a competition after applications have closed.

Unequal visibility

Learners outside established networks hear about fewer opportunities.

Limited reach

Organisers struggle to reach the right schools and students beyond their existing contacts.

Opportiva brings it all into one searchable place.

Our promise

Free to discover. Always.

Browsing, searching and viewing opportunity details costs nothing, with no paywall and no account needed. If we ever add optional services for organisers or schools, they will be clearly explained, fully optional and tested first.

The pilot

Starting in Badagry. Built for Nigeria.

We’re beginning with a local pilot in Badagry, Lagos State, working with selected schools and trusted organisers. We’ll learn what students and schools need, improve the platform, then grow to other parts of Lagos and across Nigeria.

The road ahead

From idea to tested pilot.

  1. 01

    Research

    Talk to schools, students, parents and organisers.
  2. 02

    Prototype

    Build the homepage, listings, search, filters and submission flow.
  3. 03

    Test

    Check mobile use, accessibility, privacy and listing accuracy.
  4. 04

    Pilot

    Try it with selected schools and credible organisers in Badagry.
  5. 05

    Improve & expand

    Use feedback to improve, then grow step by step.

We’re starting with a website. A mobile app may come later.

Questions

Good questions, plain answers.

Why Opportiva?

Students shouldn’t miss opportunities just because the information never reached them.

How is it different from social media?

Social media spreads announcements across countless posts. Opportiva organises educational opportunities in one searchable place, with details like deadlines and eligibility.

Is it free?

Yes. Browsing, searching and viewing opportunity details are free, with no account needed.

How do I know a listing is genuine?

Listings are reviewed, and you can report anything that looks inaccurate or suspicious.

How will students hear about Opportiva?

Through school partnerships, student ambassadors, educational organisations and community outreach.

How will Opportiva keep running?

Through verified school and organiser accounts, partnerships and optional services for institutions. Public discovery stays free.

Why start in Badagry?

It’s a community where access to information about educational opportunities can be improved. We’ll test and learn there before expanding.
